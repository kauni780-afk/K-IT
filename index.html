About EcoSphere

EcoSphere was developed as a mini project assignment for the course CFP0503 Introduction to Information Technology at UniKL MICET. The primary mission of this portal is to foster environmental awareness among students, faculty, and the public while promoting sustainable lifestyle habits.

Project Objectives

  • Enhance teamwork and basic web development skills (HTML, CSS, JS).
  • Serve as a central resource hub for Earth Day initiatives.
  • Offer interactive digital tools like quizzes and carbon calculators.

History of Earth Day

First celebrated on April 22, 1970 in the United States, Earth Day was founded by Senator Gaylord Nelson following the massive 1969 Santa Barbara oil spill to raise environmental consciousness.

1970

The Birth of the Modern Movement

20 million Americans took to the streets to demonstrate for environmental protection, leading to landmark environmental legislation.

1990

Going Global

Earth Day mobilized 200 million people across 141 countries, bringing recycling and climate protection to the global stage.

Present

World's Largest Environmental Event

Observed annually by over 1 billion people in 193+ countries to demand climate action and sustainable policies.

Key Environmental Issues

1. Climate Change & Global Warming

Rising greenhouse gas emissions (CO₂) from fossil fuel combustion trap heat in the atmosphere, driving extreme weather and melting polar ice caps.

2. Plastic Pollution

Over 8 million metric tons of plastic waste enter oceans annually. Microplastics contaminate food chains and endanger marine biodiversity.

3. Deforestation

Clearing forests for agriculture and urban development destroys wildlife habitats and significantly reduces Earth's carbon absorption capacity.

4. Air Pollution

Vehicle emissions and industrial waste degrade ambient air quality, posing severe respiratory health risks in urban areas.

Sustainable Lifestyle Practices

The 3R Concept (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)

Minimize single-use items, repurpose existing containers, and properly sort recyclable waste into designated bins.

Energy Conservation

Turn off lights and appliances when not in use. Upgrade to energy-efficient LED bulbs and smart power strips.

Green Transportation

Opt for walking, cycling, carpooling, or public transit to significantly lower your personal transport carbon footprint.

Eco-Friendly DIY Projects

Plastic Bottle Self-Watering System

Upcycle plastic bottles into an automated drip irrigation mechanism for potted houseplants.

  1. Clean plastic bottle and remove all labels.
  2. Puncture small holes into the bottle cap.
  3. Fill with water and insert inverted into plant soil.

Kitchen Waste Organic Composting

Convert fruit peels and vegetable scraps into nutrient-rich natural fertilizer at home.

  1. Collect raw organic kitchen waste.
  2. Mix with dry leaves and soil in a ventilated bin.
  3. Decompose for 4-6 weeks into rich organic compost.
